• Register provideMineralI18n() with a map of locale dictionaries.
  • Read the active locale, dictionary and translator from MI18nService (signals), or translate in templates with the mT pipe.
  • The active locale is stored in localStorage by default and restored on the next page load.
  • document.documentElement.lang follows the active locale.
  • toggleLocale() follows the order of keys in the locales object.
  • The service is optional for library components: without it they use English texts. With it, they read mineralui.* keys from your dictionaries (the same keys as MineralUI for React).
  • Dates, numbers and relative times use the active locale, then Angular's LOCALE_ID.
