• ↑ / ↓ (← / → with orientation="horizontal") move to the previous / next item, wrapping unless [loop]="false". Home and End jump to the ends.
  • Typing jumps to the next item whose text starts with what you typed; repeating one letter cycles through the matches. The text comes from the element, or from mKeyboardNavItem="…".
  • Enter — and Space in roving mode — emit (itemActivated) with the item index. The keys are consumed, so handle the action there and in (click).
  • Disabled items (:disabled or aria-disabled="true") are skipped. Escape and Tab are left to the surrounding popover or dialog.

React exposes the useKeyboardNav hook: an activeIndex counter with itemCount and no ARIA, so screen readers never hear which option is highlighted. The Angular directive finds its items itself, manages tabindex or aria-activedescendant, adds type-ahead and leaves Escape to the layer stack.
