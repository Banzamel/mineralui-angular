MPopover is the shared floating surface of Select, Autocomplete, Dropdown Menu and the pickers.

  • Escape closes the topmost layer only — a popover inside a popover, or inside a modal, closes first.
  • A press outside the popover and its anchor closes it. Popovers stay in the DOM next to their anchor, so a press inside a nested popover counts as inside its parent.
  • A widget inside that handles Escape itself (a ghost text hint, a keyboard move) calls preventDefault(), and the popover stays open until the next press.
  • Tab out of the popover closes it and focus moves on in document order (closeOnTabOut, on by default; keep m-popover right after its anchor in the markup). Turn it off for a layer that should stay open while focus visits the page.
  • initialFocus decides where focus goes on open: none (default — comboboxes keep focus in their field), first (menus, dialogs; the popover itself when nothing inside is tabbable), panel, or a given element — a template reference or an ElementRef.
  • restoreFocus (on by default): when focus was inside, it returns to the anchor on close — or to the first focusable element inside an anchor that cannot take focus itself, and also when the popover is destroyed while open. A press outside keeps focus where the press put it.
  • (closed) reports why the popover closed itself: escape, outside, tab or trigger. Setting open to false yourself emits nothing.
  • No anchorRef / onClose: [mPopoverTrigger] (or [anchor]) and [(open)] with (closed).
  • No zIndex: the top layer always stacks later layers above earlier ones.
  • No role prop with the default listbox: set the role that fits the content (menu, listbox, dialog) on m-popover, like aria-label and id.
  • closeOnTabOut is on by default (React: off, every consumer that moves focus in turns it on). The popover stays next to its anchor in the DOM, so Tab and Shift+Tab move on without being redirected through the anchor.
  • initialFocus="container" is panel here, and none is spelled out instead of leaving the prop unset.
